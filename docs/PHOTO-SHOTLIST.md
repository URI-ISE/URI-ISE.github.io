# Photos needed for the site

Members upload their own photos via pull request (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md)). The build
resizes, crops, and converts everything automatically, so upload the **largest, sharpest original** you
have and don't shrink it first. Raw iPhone HEIC files: export as JPG first (Photos → File → Export).

- Shoulders-up, facing the camera, plain light background, even light, no sunglasses or busy backgrounds.

## 5. Tips

- Landscape for rooms and projects, portrait or square for headshots. Mark tall project photos with
  `portrait: true`.
- Wipe the lens, turn on the room lights, and avoid shooting into windows.
- Screenshots: export at full resolution and crop out desktop clutter.
- Videos: MP4 under 10 MB, 10–30 seconds. Send longer or larger clips to Luke to compress.
- Only upload photos you took or have permission to use. Check with anyone recognizable in the shot.
