A major thread of the lab's work is instrumenting CNC machines with sensors and putting that
data to work: machine-learning models for predictive tool wear, digital twins of the machining
process, and data visualization that surfaces overall equipment effectiveness (OEE) for
optimization and performance improvement. Two machines anchor this work:

- **Rownd CNC lathe** — A programmable turning center the lab is using to produce
  chess pieces while automating and optimizing the workflow for complex parts that require
  4th-axis milling alongside turning.
- **Compact metal CNC** — A small metal-cutting mill producing LEGO-scale parts
  with incredibly tight clearances, pushing fixturing and tolerance control at miniature scale.
